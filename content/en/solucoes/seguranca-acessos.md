sinais:
  - Accounts of people who left the company are still active.
  - Nobody knows for sure who has access to what.
  - Permissions have piled up with every change of role.
  - Security changes are made with no record and no way back.

entregas:
  - titulo: Access and permission review
    texto: Users, groups and permissions mapped and checked against each person’s role.
  - titulo: Offboarding governance
    texto: Criteria and a routine for revoking access, including accounts unused for a defined period.
  - titulo: Hardening and controls
    texto: Firewall, web filtering, encryption, DLP and endpoint policies reviewed and adjusted.
  - titulo: Traceability
    texto: Logs, evidence and change records, always with a prior backup and the option to roll back.

ganhos:
  - Less exposure to stale access
  - Controlled, verifiable offboarding
  - Least privilege as the rule, not the exception
  - Security changes with no surprises in production
